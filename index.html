About

Bridging enterprise CRM with autonomous AI

I work on the operational bottlenecks that healthcare and insurance organizations run into once their CRM stops being a system of record and has to become a system of action — claims routing, prior authorization, patient onboarding, SLA-driven service queues.

Across engagements with Accenture, LTIMindtree, and Tech Mahindra, I've built that layer with Dynamics 365, custom C#/.NET plugins, JavaScript client APIs, and Power Platform automation — the parts that don't show up in a demo but decide whether the system holds up at 50,000 transactions a day.

My current focus is what comes after the workflow: orchestrating autonomous agents through Microsoft Copilot Studio and Azure OpenAI, so routine casework gets handled by a digital coworker instead of a queue.

Consulting backgrounds: Accenture, LTIMindtree, Tech Mahindra, NTT DATA Services.

Domains: Healthcare, Insurance.

Based in Bengaluru, India.
